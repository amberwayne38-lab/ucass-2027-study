简答题英文答题模板（原创）

1. Claim: Identify the author's distinctive contribution or the work's defining feature.

2. Textual evidence: Discuss two specific formal or thematic features with accurate references to the work.

3. Context: Explain the relevant literary movement and historical conditions.

4. Significance: Evaluate the work's place in literary history without vague praise.

该框架为原创备考建议，不是教材原文或某校官方评分标准。
