英国复辟与18世纪 · Picaresque novel / epistolary novel / mock-epic

A picaresque narrative typically follows a socially marginal protagonist through loosely connected adventures that expose the habits and institutions of different social groups. Defoe’s Moll Flanders adapts this episodic pattern through Moll’s accounts of marriage, theft and transportation. A useful comparison is Richardson’s Pamela: its letters make the act of writing part of the story and give readers access to Pamela’s immediate fears and calculations. For mock-epic, Pope’s The Rape of the Lock supplies a different formal mechanism: elevated epic conventions magnify the cutting of a lock of hair and expose the values of fashionable society.

流浪汉小说靠游历串起社会横截面，书信体靠文书安排视角与叙述时点，mock-epic 靠语体与事件规模的错位产生讽刺。

英国浪漫主义 · Wordsworth’s Tintern Abbey

In Tintern Abbey, Wordsworth traces a change in the speaker’s relation to nature. The landscape first returns to him in memory during his absence, when its remembered forms relieve the strain of urban life. On revisiting the Wye, he contrasts his earlier, intensely physical delight with a mature perception that can include human suffering. His address to Dorothy then projects the same restorative power into another person’s future. Nature thus acts through perception, memory and affection, and the poem makes the speaker’s claim about its moral power emerge from a particular history of visits and losses.

短答顺序：离开后记忆中的作用 → 重访中的变化 → 对 Dorothy 的祝愿。每一步写作用机制。

英国维多利亚时期 · Realism in Dickens and Hardy

A comparative answer can begin with the institutions that shape a character’s choices. In Bleak House, the Chancery lawsuit consumes resources and extends across generations. The alternation between Esther’s retrospective narration and the present-tense narrator gives access both to domestic experience and to a wider institutional network. In Tess of the d’Urbervilles, the threshing-machine episode joins concrete details of agricultural labour to Tess’s exhaustion and economic dependence. A comparison of the two scenes shows how different narrative methods make institutions and material conditions perceptible through their effects on individual lives.

论述结构：提出共同机制 → Dickens 的两种叙述位置 → Hardy 的劳动场景 → 比较形式怎样让社会压力可见。

英美现代主义 · Hemingway and the iceberg principle

In Hills Like White Elephants, the couple’s conversation centres on an operation whose nature is left implicit. Repeated assurances and replies that shift the subject make the disagreement legible without giving direct access to either character’s thoughts. The landscape and the railway junction place the conversation within a setting organized around alternatives and departure. Hemingway’s omissions therefore create an interpretive task: the reader must infer the stakes and the unequal pressure behind apparently ordinary speech.

简答结构：界定省略 → 指出具体被省掉的信息 → 用反复、转话题或场景解释读者如何推断。

美国浪漫主义 · Thoreau’s Walden

In the chapter Where I Lived, and What I Lived For, Thoreau presents the move to Walden Pond as an experiment in discovering what a life requires. His figurative language turns deliberate living into an active process of testing and reducing ordinary habits. The accounting in Economy gives that position a material dimension by asking what comforts cost in labour and time. A discussion of the experiment can also consider its local setting near Concord: solitude provides a vantage point from which to reconsider social habits while the writer remains in contact with other people.

论述结构：从题面意象提出主张 → 连接 Economy 的成本核算 → 结合 Concord 的地理与社会关系限定结论。

美国现实主义与自然主义 · Regional realism and naturalism

In Adventures of Huckleberry Finn, Huck’s vernacular voice locates him socially and exposes a gap between the moral rules he has learned and his experience of Jim. In Sister Carrie, Dreiser follows the changing effects of wages, consumer desire and employment on the opportunities available to Carrie and Hurstwood. Both novels make social conditions visible through concrete details. The first example foregrounds voice and the contradictions of local moral authority; the second foregrounds the accumulation of pressures that constrain individual choices.

辨析写法：同样写环境，分别追踪叙述声音与社会经济压力。结尾回到文本如何塑造人物选择。

英国中古时期 · The Canterbury Tales：叙事框架和叙述者

Chaucer’s pilgrimage brings people from different occupations into a shared narrative space. The General Prologue introduces the pilgrims through selective details of dress, speech and conduct, while the tales allow them to present their own accounts of experience. The Wife of Bath’s appeal to experience in her prologue, for example, makes her a participant in a debate about marriage and authority. A strong account of the frame should connect social variety to the competing voices that the telling of tales makes possible.

先写框架怎样聚集人物，再写人物如何通过讲述争夺解释权。可与 estates satire 和 frame narrative 一起复习。

英国文艺复兴 · Marlowe：blank verse 与 dramatic ambition

Marlowe’s use of blank verse gives dramatic shape to the aspirations of his protagonists. In Doctor Faustus, the opening survey of established disciplines moves through a series of judgments until Faustus turns to magic. The verse organizes his reasoning and makes his confidence audible. His final soliloquy gives the same dramatic form a different function: the pressure of passing time breaks up his plans and intensifies his appeals. The contrast between these scenes links changes in speech to the tragic movement of the play.

术语定义可先写 unrhymed iambic pentameter，简答则结合开场和尾声比较语气、句法与人物状态。

《英国文学-文艺复兴时期》PDF 第 8–14 页。

英国文艺复兴 · Twelfth Night：dramatic irony 与 mistaken identity

Viola’s disguise as Cesario creates an uneven distribution of knowledge: the audience knows something that Orsino and Olivia do not. Olivia’s attraction to Cesario therefore produces dramatic irony before Sebastian’s arrival. When the twins are confused, mistaken identity turns this difference in knowledge into a visible problem for the other characters. Recognition in the last act resolves the confusion about the twins, while Viola’s earlier role as Cesario has already shown how strongly desire can depend on dress, speech and social role.

先区分观众知道什么、角色以为什么，再写重认解决了什么。mistaken identity 是情节机制，dramatic irony 是信息差造成的效果。

美国19世纪诗歌 · Dickinson：personification 与诗节节奏

In Because I could not stop for Death, Dickinson personifies Death as a courteous figure who arrives in a carriage. The journey passes scenes associated with different stages of life and ends at a house whose description suggests a grave. These changes give a social gesture, the offer of a ride, an unsettling temporal scale. The final stanza looks back from a perspective of centuries and revises the pace implied by the earlier journey. A close reading can connect this shift to the poem’s compact stanzas and its pauses.

先写人格化怎样建立日常语气，再定位坟墓意象和最后一节的时间转折。

美国战后文学 · Morrison：Beloved 与历史记忆

In Beloved, the haunted house and Beloved’s arrival give a continuing presence to events that Sethe struggles to articulate. The novel returns to the past through different voices and through scenes that acquire new significance when revisited. This narrative structure makes remembering a collective and contested process. In an essay, the women’s gathering outside 124 offers a specific point of comparison with Sethe’s earlier isolation: it connects the problem of living with the past to communal intervention.

论述从叙事次序和视角切入，联系 124 的两组场景，再解释个人记忆与共同体的关系。
